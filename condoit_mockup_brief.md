
# Condoit Website Mockup: Creative Brief & Build Directive

## Project Goal  
Rebuild Station A’s website structure for **Condoit**, replacing all copy, colors, and branding elements to reflect Condoit’s mission:

> **Digitize electrical infrastructure. Enable safer, compliant, lower-cost facilities. Connect facility owners with electrical contractors to build recurring preventative maintenance revenue.**

---

## Audience & Use Cases

| **Audience** | **What They Get From Condoit** |
|--------------|-------------------------------|
| **Facility Owners & Operators** | Fast documentation of electrical systems, compliance with NFPA 70B/70E, reduced operational & new construction costs. |
| **Electrical Contractors** | New revenue model: recurring maintenance instead of one-off installs. Access to a steady stream of facilities needing service. |
| **Partners (Utilities, Distributors, Trade Orgs)** | Connect their networks to Condoit’s platform, help facilities get compliant, strengthen relationships. |

---

## Brand Voice & Tone

- **Field-first:** Speak directly to the realities of facilities and contractors.
- **Straightforward & No-Nonsense:** Avoid fluff. Use plain language with bite where appropriate.
- **Bold & Confident:** Condoit isn’t optional—it’s the future of electrical maintenance.
- **Minimalist but Impactful:** Get to the point fast, but don’t lose gravity.

---

## Color Palette

| **Color** | **HEX** | **Usage** |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Hot Black** | #0B1215 | Headlines, primary text |
| **Dim Grey** | #586266 | Body text |
| **Breaker Grey** | #C5CCD3 | Lines, strokes, muted text |
| **Neutral Grey** | #F2F2F2 | Background elements |
| **Neutral White** | #FAFAFA | Background |
| **Hot Red** | #F93535 | Interactive elements (buttons, links) |
| **Bittersweet** | #FF5B5B | Hover states |

---

## Typography

- **Primary Font:** **Geist**
- **Fallback for Presentations:** **Inter**
- **Type Guidelines:**  
  - Body: 10–12pt, line spacing +4pt  
  - Headlines: Scalable, but use 140pt Geist with -20 tracking for major hero text.

## Visual & UX Guidelines

- **Minimalist layout:** No clutter, focus on key messaging and clean interactions.
- **Colors:** Replace all Station A greens/blues with Condoit’s **black/grey/red palette**.
- **Buttons:** Use **Hot Red (#F93535)** for primary actions, **Bittersweet (#FF5B5B)** for hover states.
- **Imagery:** Focus on electrical systems, schematics, QR codes, single-line diagrams, fieldwork—not solar panels or batteries.
- **Logos:** Use Condoit’s “C” conduit bolt mark where applicable.

---

## Deliverables

- A **mockup landing page** reflecting this structure and branding.
- All pages should reflect **Condoit’s voice, color scheme, and typography**.
- Output can be in **Figma, HTML, or image format**, whichever is most useful for rapid prototyping.
