
# Condoit "How It Works" Page Brief

## Purpose & Placement

This page will live behind the **"How It Works"** button on Condoit’s main homepage.  
It is a **deep dive into Condoit’s process**, guiding facility owners, operators, and contractors through how the platform works—from uploading facilities to contractor matching.

## Brand Voice & Visual Standards

- Use **Condoit’s brand guidelines** (Hot Black, Hot Red, Geist font, minimalist layout).
- Maintain a **direct, no-nonsense tone** with an emphasis on simplicity and action.
- Avoid sustainability or clean energy language—focus on **electrical system safety, compliance, documentation, and maintenance**.

## Page Content & Copy

### **Header Section**

## We simplify how you manage electrical systems.

Our platform helps facility owners and contractors evaluate, document, and maintain electrical infrastructure—digitizing records, ensuring compliance, and unlocking long-term cost reduction in the fastest and most efficient way.

### **Step 1: Portfolio Evaluation**

## Get a prioritized report of your entire portfolio—for free.

Upload a simple address list. Condoit will analyze your facilities and return a detailed report showing:

- Equipment quality & risk level  
- Documentation status  
- Compliance gaps (70B/70E readiness)  
- Ease of capture (how quickly each facility can be documented)  
- Estimated scope of work by site

**[Get Free Portfolio Evaluation]**

---

### **Step 2: Capture & Compliance Study**

## Turn estimates into action with real-world data.

Add your own building data—or use Condoit to dispatch a contractor for on-site capture. We’ll document your electrical system, update your single-line diagram, and perform a compliance review.  

Your portfolio report will instantly update with verified data, replacing estimates with real insights.

**Already have a contractor you trust?**  
No problem—you can invite them into the Condoit network at no cost. They’ll get access to the tools and workflows to capture your data and help you stay compliant.

**[Request Capture & Compliance Study]**

### **Step 3: Service Proposals & Contractor Matching**

## Easily collect preventative maintenance proposals.

Once your system is captured, Condoit makes it simple to get competitive proposals from trusted contractors in our network—or from contractors you already work with.

List your facility’s service needs in the Condoit Marketplace to receive bids for:

- Preventative maintenance contracts  
- Compliance monitoring and testing  
- Ongoing system documentation and updates  
- Emergency and pull-through work

**[List a Facility for Service Proposals]**

### **Step 4: Proposal Review & Decision Support**

## Pick the best contractor for your needs—with full transparency.

Condoit standardizes all incoming proposals so you can compare options easily. We levelize costs, services, and scopes side by side, helping you choose the right partner with confidence.

No guesswork. No hidden costs. Just clear, actionable comparisons.
