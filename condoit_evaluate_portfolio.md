
# Condoit "Evaluate Your Portfolio" Page Brief

## Purpose & Placement

This page will live behind the **"Get Free Portfolio Evaluation"** button on the Condoit homepage and How It Works page.

It allows facility owners and operators to upload a list of facilities and receive a **free, prioritized report** of their electrical systems.

## Brand Voice & Visual Standards

- Use **Condoit’s brand guidelines** (Hot Black, Hot Red, Geist font, minimalist layout).
- Maintain a **direct, field-first tone**—this is about **electrical system health, compliance, and documentation**, not clean energy.
- Keep it **simple and action-oriented**.

---

## Page Content & Copy

## **Evaluate Your Portfolio**

Condoit offers the simplest and fastest way to understand the electrical health of your facilities—across your entire portfolio.

Upload a list of sites and we’ll return a **prioritized report** showing:

- Documentation gaps  
- Compliance risks (NFPA 70B / 70E)  
- Equipment quality & condition estimates  
- Ease of capture & service readiness  

---

### **Address List Requirements**

- List **one facility per row**.  
- Each row must include **Street, City, State, Zip** in the same cell.  
- Eligible buildings include **commercial, industrial, multi-family, municipal, and nonprofit facilities**.
