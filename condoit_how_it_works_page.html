We simplify how you manage electrical systems.

Our platform helps facility owners and contractors evaluate, document, and maintain electrical infrastructure—digitizing records, ensuring compliance, and unlocking long-term cost reduction in the fastest and most efficient way.

Step 1: Portfolio Evaluation

Get a prioritized report of your entire portfolio—for free.

Upload a simple address list. Condoit will analyze your facilities and return a detailed report showing:

  • Equipment quality & risk level
  • Documentation status
  • Compliance gaps (70B/70E readiness)
  • Ease of capture (how quickly each facility can be documented)
  • Estimated scope of work by site
